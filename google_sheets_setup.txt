GOOGLE SHEETS CONTROL PANEL — SIMPLE SETUP

WHY THIS IS BETTER
After the one-time setup, you will not edit code every day.
You will only edit a Google Sheet.

WHAT YOU WILL CHANGE IN THE SHEET
- unlocked: FALSE keeps a card locked.
- unlocked: TRUE releases it.
- heading: short heading beneath the scratch layer.
- message: the main clue/message.
- closing: the final line.

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Go to Google Sheets and create a blank spreadsheet.
2. In Google Sheets choose File > Import > Upload.
3. Upload 30_Before_30_Card_Control.csv.
4. Choose Replace spreadsheet and import.
5. Do not rename or delete the headings in Row 1.
6. Choose File > Share > Publish to web.
7. Select the sheet and choose Comma-separated values (.csv).
8. Click Publish.
9. Copy the published CSV link.
10. Send that link back in this ChatGPT conversation.
11. The website will be updated once to use your sheet.

AFTER THAT
1. Open your Google Sheet.
2. Find the card you want.
3. Type the heading, message and closing.
4. Change FALSE to TRUE.
5. The published website will read the change automatically.
6. On iPhone, close and reopen the page, or refresh it.

IMPORTANT
Anyone who somehow obtains the published CSV link could read the sheet.
Do not put private passwords, banking details or highly confidential information in it.
Birthday clues and messages are generally suitable.
