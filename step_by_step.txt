STEP-BY-STEP: PUBLISH NOW AND ADD CARDS LATER

CURRENT SETUP
- Access code: CHANEL30
- Only Card 1 is unlocked.
- Cards 2–30 are locked and may remain empty.
- Progress text reads: 1 / 30 adventures.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
1. Open index.html on your computer.
2. Enter CHANEL30.
3. Check the envelope, letter, wall and Card 1.
4. Test the scratch card.

PUBLISH FREE ON NETLIFY
1. Unzip the downloaded website ZIP.
2. Keep every file together inside one folder.
3. Sign in to Netlify.
4. Open Netlify Drop / manual deploy.
5. Drag the UNZIPPED FOLDER onto the upload area.
6. Wait for the netlify.app link.
7. Open that public link on your iPhone.
8. Test the password and Card 1 before sending the link.

ADD OR UNLOCK A CARD LATER
1. Keep the original website folder safely on your computer.
2. Open card-data.js with a plain-text editor.
3. Find the relevant card number.
4. Add its heading, message and closing.
5. Change "unlocked": false to "unlocked": true.
6. Save card-data.js.
7. In index.html, change card-data.js?v=1 to card-data.js?v=2.
   On the next update, change v=2 to v=3, and so on.
   This helps iPhones load the newest card information instead of an older cached copy.
8. Log in to the SAME Netlify site.
9. Open its Deploys page / manual deploy area.
10. Drag the complete updated folder into the deployment area.
11. The same public link will show the new version.
12. Test it on your own phone before telling Chanel the next card is available.

IMPORTANT
Do not create a new Netlify site every day. Update the existing site so the link stays the same.
