HOW TO PUT THIS UPDATE ON YOUR EXISTING NETLIFY SITE

1. Download and unzip the updated ZIP.
2. Sign in to Netlify.
3. Open the SAME site/project you already published.
4. Click Deploys.
5. Scroll to the manual deploy / deploy dropzone near the bottom.
6. Drag the entire unzipped website folder into that dropzone.
7. Wait until the new deploy says Published.
8. Open your existing public link on your iPhone.
9. Refresh Safari. If the old version remains, close the tab and reopen the link.

Do not create a new site. Updating the existing site keeps the same URL.
